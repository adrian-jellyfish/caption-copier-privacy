# Caption Copier Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 20, 2026
**Publisher:** Adrian Jellyfish

Caption Copier shows the captions produced by Windows Live Captions and lets you paste a selected part of them into another app. This policy explains what information the app and its purchase website handle and what is done with it.

## Summary

- The app works entirely on your device. It does not connect to the internet.
- The app collects no personal information. It contains no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking.
- If you buy a license, the purchase is made on our website through a payment provider. We receive your email address and the details of the purchase, and use them only to give you your license and to support you.
- We never sell personal information.

## Information the app handles

**Caption text.** The app reads the caption text that Windows Live Captions displays, using the Windows UI Automation interface. The text is kept in the app's memory only while the app is running so that you can select and paste it. It is not saved to disk and is deleted when you close the app.

**Clipboard.** When you paste, the app briefly puts the selected caption text on the Windows clipboard and sends Ctrl+V (and, if you turn on that option, Enter) to the app you are typing in. About half a second later it puts your previous clipboard content back. The app does not otherwise read or keep clipboard content.

**Keyboard and mouse.** The app registers one keyboard shortcut that you choose, so that it can react when you press it. If you choose middle-click pasting instead, the app watches only for the middle mouse button; all other mouse input passes through untouched. The app does not record keystrokes, mouse movement or anything you type.

**Settings.** Your preferences (such as the shortcut, language, theme, font size and window position) are saved in a settings file in your Windows user profile on your device. This file contains no caption text and is never sent anywhere.

**Free trial and license.** The app saves the time you first started it (to know when the 3-day free trial ends) and, once you activate one, your license key, in a file in your Windows user profile on your device. A license key contains the email address it was issued to, the purchase identifier and the date of issue. The app checks the key on your device, without contacting any server, and shows the email address in Settings. This file is never sent anywhere.

The "Buy a license" button only opens our purchase website in your web browser.

## Purchases

Licenses are sold on our purchase website (caption-copier.adrian20020105.workers.dev), which runs on Cloudflare.

**Card payments** are processed by Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record for card orders. Paddle collects the information needed for the payment and for taxes, such as your name, email address, country or address, and payment card details, and handles it under [Paddle's privacy policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy). We never see your full card details. From Paddle we receive your email address and the details of the transaction (such as the transaction number, product, amount and status).

**Cryptocurrency payments** are processed by NOWPayments. You enter your email address on our website; we store it together with an order number and the order's payment status in our order database (on Cloudflare). NOWPayments processes the payment under [its privacy policy](https://nowpayments.io/privacy-policy) and tells us the payment's status. Payments on a blockchain are public by the nature of the blockchain.

**Our website** does not use cookies, analytics or advertising. Its host, Cloudflare, processes technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the website, under [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). The card checkout is provided by Paddle's script, which may set its own cookies under Paddle's policy.

**How we use purchase information:** to issue your license key, to show it to you again or resend it if you lose it, to handle refunds and support requests, and to keep records required by law. We do not send marketing email.

**How long we keep it:** for as long as needed for these purposes, including the legal record-keeping periods for sales. You can ask us to delete your information at any time; we will delete it except for what we must keep by law.

## Information shared with others

The app shares nothing. For purchases, information is shared only with the providers named above (Paddle, NOWPayments and Cloudflare), as needed to process and deliver your purchase, and with authorities where the law requires it.

## Windows Live Captions and the Microsoft Store

Caption Copier reads text from Windows Live Captions, a feature of Windows. How Live Captions processes audio is governed by Microsoft's privacy statement, not by this policy. The Microsoft Store may also collect information about installs and app usage under Microsoft's privacy statement. The publisher does not control either of these.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases are intended for adults or with the permission of a parent or guardian.
