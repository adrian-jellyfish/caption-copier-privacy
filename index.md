# Caption Copier Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 29, 2026
**Publisher:** Adrian Jellyfish

Caption Copier shows the captions produced by Windows Live Captions and lets you paste a selected part of them into another app. This policy explains what information the app handles and what it does with it.

## Summary

- Caption Copier works entirely on your device.
- It does not connect to the internet.
- It does not collect, store, share or sell personal information.
- It contains no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking.

## Information the app handles

**Caption text.** The app reads the caption text that Windows Live Captions displays, using the Windows UI Automation interface. The text is kept in the app's memory only while the app is running so that you can select and paste it. It is not saved to disk and is deleted when you close the app.

**Clipboard.** When you paste, the app briefly puts the selected caption text on the Windows clipboard and sends Ctrl+V (and, if you turn on that option, Enter) to the app you are typing in. About half a second later it puts your previous clipboard content back. The app does not otherwise read or keep clipboard content.

**Keyboard and mouse.** The app registers one keyboard shortcut that you choose, so that it can react when you press it. If you choose middle-click pasting instead, the app watches only for the middle mouse button; all other mouse input passes through untouched. The app does not record keystrokes, mouse movement or anything you type.

**Settings.** Your preferences (such as the shortcut, language, theme, font size and window position) are saved in a settings file in your Windows user profile on your device. This file contains no caption text and is never sent anywhere.

## Information shared with others

None. Caption Copier does not transmit any information to the publisher or to any third party.

## Windows Live Captions and the Microsoft Store

Caption Copier reads text from Windows Live Captions, a feature of Windows. How Live Captions processes audio is governed by Microsoft's privacy statement, not by this policy. The Microsoft Store may also collect information about installs and app usage under Microsoft's privacy statement. The publisher does not control either of these.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
